## Intent Legend
- **Informational** – user wants to learn or explore ideas
- **Commercial** – user is comparing options before visiting
- **Local** – user wants a nearby place right now
- **Navigational** – user is looking for a specific business or location

## Method
1. Seed keywords from business type + area + landmark.
2. Expanded using Google autosuggest, "People also ask" and related searches.
3. Mapped each keyword to one page to avoid keyword cannibalization.
