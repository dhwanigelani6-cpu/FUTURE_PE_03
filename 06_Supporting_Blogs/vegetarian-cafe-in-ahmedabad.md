## What to Expect at a Vegetarian Cafe

- A fully vegetarian menu, so no confusion for the table
- A mix of coffee, beverages, snacks, meals and desserts
- A relaxed place to meet friends or family

## How to Choose a Good Vegetarian Cafe

1. **Menu variety:** more than one cuisine is a good sign
2. **Hygiene and cleanliness:** clean tables and a tidy kitchen area
3. **Seating options:** indoor, outdoor or rooftop
4. **Location:** easy to reach from home or college
