## Rules Followed
- Every supporting post links to the pillar.
- The pillar links to every supporting post.
- Anchor text is natural and varied (no exact-match repetition).
- 2-3 internal links per post, placed where they help the reader.

## Local SEO Add-ons
- Mention area, landmark and road in each post: Chandkheda, Zundal, Sardar Patel Ring Road.
- End every post with a Google Maps + phone CTA.
- Keep business name, address and phone identical to the Google Business Profile (NAP consistency).
- Add LocalBusiness / Restaurant schema on the website [optional, future step].
